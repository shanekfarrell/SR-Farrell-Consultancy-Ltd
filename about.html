About

Experience spanning journalism, technology, and global markets.

Background

The convergence of content, technology, and strategy

SR Farrell Consultancy operates at the convergence of content strategy, AI automation, and international business development. Based in Portugal with Irish registration, the practice serves clients globally with particular expertise in web3 and emerging technology sectors.

The consultancy draws on over a decade of high-stakes communications work, including coordinating 45-person distributed journalist networks in conflict zones and partnering with organizations like International Medical Corps. This background informs an approach to content and automation that emphasizes clarity, reliability, and cross-cultural effectiveness.

Recent engagements include serving as AI Hub & Content Lead at Myosin, a selective crypto marketing DAO (accepting fewer than 5% of applicants) working with tier-one clients including Ripple, NEAR, EtherFuse, BIO.XYZ, and Gnosis. Previous work with Coin Bureau, the world's largest cryptocurrency media platform, involved translating complex blockchain concepts into accessible content for millions of global viewers.
